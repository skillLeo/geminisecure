E · Notes for the build
The brand mark is two circles, never a shield
Two circles at r 12.5, centres at cx 15 and cx 25 in a 40×40 viewBox, with mix-blend-mode:multiply on the right one. That overlap is the Gemini twins and it is the whole identity. Four authorised colour pairs are set out in the Foundation.
Icons carry their own stroke weight
Sidebar navigation is 1.8, KPI tiles are 1.7, activity rows are 1.6, input glyphs are 1.6, and the settings cog is 1.5. These are not interchangeable — a uniform 2px stroke reads visibly heavier and is the fastest way to make the set look re-drawn.
Semantic colour is locked
Navy and amber are the brand and may be themed per client. Success #16A34A, danger #B91C1C and warning amber are meanings, not decoration — a community that themes the product must never be able to make denied look like verified.
Statutory figures still need your accountant
The payroll and payslip documents carry rates and totals that are structurally correct but not yet reconciled against current TAJ tables. Confirm them before the first live run — approving a payroll run files real money.
